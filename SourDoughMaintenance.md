# Sour dough boule receipe

### Ingredients
White bread flour, Wholemeal bread flour, Rye flour, salt, starter, water, mixed seeds (optional).

### Utensils
Baking bowel, 250ml glass jar, wooden spoon, prooving basket, dutch oven dish, 1L glass jar, digital scales.

### Terms
**Levain:** When starter is mixed with flour and water

## Starter maintenance
1. Weigh out how much starter you have and add it to your 250ml jar where you plan on storing the starter, no more than 50g.
2. The general rule for maintenance is 1:1:1 starter:flour:water. The starter seems to like a mix of all three flour types.
**Example**
Your starter weighs 40g.
Add 40g water.
Divide 40g by 3 = 13.33g. For simplicity add 13g, 13g, 14g of each of the flours.

3. Mix together well and leave in the fridge with the lid very loose to allow gas exchange.
